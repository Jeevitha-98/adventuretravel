Frequent Traveler Queries

How is emergency medical evacuation managed?

All active bookings include standard secondary search-and-rescue satellite telemetry routing hooks. In an emergency, basecamp dispatch immediately deploys regional helicopter support vectors using your live GPS location data.

Can I adjust my packing weight allowance later?

Yes. Rucksack parameters can be re-calibrated natively inside the Gear Packing panel up to 48 hours prior to trail check-in. Excess logistics demands may trigger porter trust fee adjustments.

What happens if weather cancels a route?

If meteorological sensors signal unsafe conditions (e.g., avalanche risk, rapid flooding), the outfitter triggers a protective route hold. Your escrow funds are held securely until the transit window stabilizes or alternative routes are mapped.
